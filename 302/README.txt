Ravedio 302 | Ponytail bandana trial

The triangular paisley scarf comes over the base of the ponytail.
Its two rear folds turn inward and gather into a knot beneath the
hanging hair, with two short fabric ends. The tail and elastic sit
slightly higher than in 301 to make space for this routing. The tail's
shape and the face-framing locks use the existing Ponytail artwork.

The previous side-knot fit is available through Previous fit. The
trial uses the same fitted shape in Brown and Ombré. Browser checks
cover eight angles, three hair selections, light and dark cloth, the
Sprout, the Cloth toggle and 36 surrounding catalogue combinations.
The export check found no scarf-to-hair intersections in either baked
hair finish. Two tiny cloth self-contact pairs at the left temple hem
were recorded during mesh review.

Rebuild locally from the complete 301 preview and generated GLBs.
Run Blender with 302/tools/fit.py in background mode. The four revised
ordinary GLBs are generated in 302/assets. To store them off the art
drive, make 302/assets a directory junction before running Blender.
Set CAPTURE_DIR and run node 302/tools/review.cjs while the preview is
served. Set PREVIEW_ORIGIN if using a host other than localhost:9251.
