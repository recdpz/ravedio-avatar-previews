Ravedio 282 | Curtain bangs refinement

This bare-head study follows 281 B. The front lobes are sculpted into the
same shell as the temples. The fringe lifts off the forehead and returns
at the ends, while a shallow part and two swept ridges guide the highlights.
The original B remains available side by side at matched camera, lighting,
clothing and color. Fitted headwear and production color variants are pending.

Reproduce
With the local 270 asset cache available, run Blender 5.2 in background mode
with --python 282/tools/build_curtain.py. Serve the artwork root and run
282/tools/capture.cjs with Playwright and Chromium to regenerate the views.
