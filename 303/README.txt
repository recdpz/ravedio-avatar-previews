Ravedio 303 | Gathered ponytail bandana

The crown panel continues through both side gathers as one cloth mesh
with shared vertices, normals and print at the join. The lower knot is
rounded and sits just off center under the hanging ponytail so more of
it is visible from the rear. The existing Ponytail fit is reused.

The exported crown and gathers form one closed component. The scarf
clears both fitted hair finishes and the scalp. The two small cloth
self-contact pairs at the left temple hem are inherited from 302. The
browser comparison includes eight angles, the brown and ombre fits,
light cloth, Sprout and the cloth toggle, and 36 surrounding fits.

Rebuild locally with the 302 catalogue and generated assets available.
Run Blender in background mode with 303/tools/fit.py. The new bandana
GLB is generated under 303/assets. Run 303/tools/review.cjs with the
preview running and CAPTURE_DIR set to save the comparison views.
