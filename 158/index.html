How I would build this

Use a simple rounded torso beneath separately modeled tops. Keep a fixed shorts mesh per build, with a clear waistband and two leg openings. A crop top can expose the midriff; a longer shirt overlaps the same shorts. This avoids making every feminine torso permanently look like a dress.

Start with Bandeau, Strappy crop and T-shirt to establish the fitting surfaces, then expand. Tops should stay recognizable in small portraits through neckline, straps, coverage and length. The triangle top should remain abstract, without realistic breast detail. Shared outfits can use restrained build-specific tailoring.

There are eight distinct top families across these sheets. They are illustrative choices, not assigned option IDs or a new wire layout. The deployed three-byte profile has no dedicated shirt field; any encoded top selector needs a separate contract decision. Fixed bottoms do not require their own selector.

Review limits: the source is generated imagery. Minor changes in scale, eye placement, cloth texture and seams occur across views. Back, top, full garment clearance, saved selections and exports still need native modeling checks. Fabric detail would be simplified to match the final character style.

Construction reference: RaveFix triangle top and high-waisted shorts. Used for garment construction only, not body proportions.
