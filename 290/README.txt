Ravedio 290 | Rounded long fringe

The profile follows 289 A and reaches the length of 289 B behind the
shoulders. The lower locks follow sculpted grooves and split into
individual, tapered ends below the nape. The surface is connected
at their roots. The brow fringe keeps the spacing of 289 A and has
a rounder edge and a closer fit to the forehead.

This is a bare-head review. Headwear and production color fits are pending.

Reproduce
With the local 270 asset cache available, run Blender 5.2 in background
mode with --python 290/tools/build_locks.py. Serve the artwork root and
run 290/tools/capture.cjs with Playwright and Chromium.
