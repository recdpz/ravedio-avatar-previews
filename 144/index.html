A more playful cartoon direction

A cohesive rethink of the head, torso and hair. I recommend the refined A · Soft rounded direction: a softer oval head, compact rounded body, shorter leg blobs and glossy sculpted hair.

What changes in this direction

  • A softly flattened oval head gives the face a friendlier surface than a perfect ball.
  • A compact torso, softer top and shorter legs make the whole character feel more intentional. The feminine build keeps a gentle skirt outline.
  • A tiny head gap and hands close to the torso keep the floating idea cohesive.
  • Broad, curved hair locks replace realistic fibers. A few shallow grooves and restrained satin highlights preserve detail when zoomed into the face.

Concept images, not new Blender geometry. These were generated with the built-in image tool and refined in a second pass. The angles illustrate the intended silhouettes; they do not certify a fitted 3D model. The curl example still reads rather wavy, and the swept rear hair needs a calmer arrangement in modeling. Our current models and native hair remain preserved.

The four existing skin pigments and futuristic polish remain the intended palette. The illustrations approximate the finish; the exact saved pigments will be used in 3D. The hairstyle thumbnails show how existing identities could translate into the new cartoon language.
