Ravedio 272 | Wider bandana and fuller tied ends

The masculine band is about a third wider at the forehead than 271. It
retains the exact original hairstyles, with surface clearance adjusted
for the volume of the hair. The rear wraps are wider too. A rounded square
knot and two fuller, asymmetric cloth ends continue from the actual wrap.

Ponytail and Space Buns use a shorter folded kerchief tied below the updo.
The raised front fold covers part of the front crown. A continuous wrap
replaces the cutout beside the ponytail and the pinched panel between the buns.
The ponytail knot sits slightly off center so its cloth ends clear the tail.
The other feminine hairstyles retain the full triangular crown scarf.
All receive the fuller knot and coordinated tails. The two print atlases
from 271 retain their original resolution, with a reseated Sprout per fit.

Review

All 16 hairstyles were captured in six angles. Browser checks confirmed
exact underlying hair and braid components with Default and Ombre, and
tested Cloth and headwear color controls. Geometry checks confirm the rear
gather meets the wrap, each tail meets the knot, and the full exported
ponytail kerchief and knot clear the tail. Six sampled non-bandana fits
resolve to their exact earlier assets.

Reproduce
Build the 270 generated assets and the 271 prints. Run Blender 5.2 with
--background --python tools/build_bandanas.py, then run tools/verify_assets.py
and tools/review.cjs. The 271 builder supplies the existing print materials,
triangular scarf surface and cloth geometry helpers.
