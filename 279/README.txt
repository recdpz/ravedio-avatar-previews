Ravedio 279 | Layered fringe

The front of the crown and the wispy ends share a continuous surface.
Eighteen tapered sections grow out of a slightly uneven crown edge,
following the crown tangent into an outward roll and then back toward
the forehead. The open spaces between sections leave the forehead visible.
Closed hats use a shallower lift under their rims.

Review
The early renders showed a bright ridge and excessive lift over the
forehead. Later passes blended the crown slope into the roots, varied
the ends and moderated the profile. The comparison includes front,
three-quarter, profile and crown views alongside 278.

The browser review covered eight angles with every headwear selection,
Ombré switching and a 120-pixel portrait. The export checks confirm
geometry parity across hair colors and pashmina clearance with all
eight tops. The Space Buns entries reuse the 276 assets.

Reproduce
Prepare the 270 generated cache and 274 and 276 assets. Run Blender 5.2
in background mode with --python 279/tools/layer_fringe.py. Run
275/tools/verify_assets.py -- 279 with Blender. Run 275/tools/review.cjs
with REVIEW_REVISION=279 and CAPTURE_DIR set while the local artwork
server is available.
