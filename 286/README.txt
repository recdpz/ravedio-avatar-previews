Ravedio 286 | Soft brow fringe with individual locks

The original 281 A defines the coverage and side silhouette. Individual
fringe strips share the crown geometry and separate near the brows. A common
radial profile keeps the surface continuous through the split. The rear
hem is slightly smoother. Both previews use matching camera, lighting,
clothing and color. Headwear fits and production color variants are pending.

Reproduce
With the local 270 asset cache available, run Blender 5.2 in background mode
with --python 286/tools/build_fringe.py. Serve the artwork root and run
286/tools/capture.cjs with Playwright and Chromium to regenerate the views.
